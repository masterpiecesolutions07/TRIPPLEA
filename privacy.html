    Privacy notice

    What Tripple A asks for, and where those details are kept.

    What the forms ask for

    Registration asks for your name, email, phone, country, experience, attendance mode, price plan, and how you heard about the mentorship. Contact and custom-price forms ask for name, email, phone, and a message.

    Where it is stored

    Your profile, a hash of your password, your theme, and which updates you have opened are kept in this browser. This version of the site does not send them to a server.

    Passwords

    The site stores a salt and a SHA-256 hash, not the password you typed. A future live system will hash passwords on a server.

    Cohort list

    The footer email box keeps your address in this browser for cohort news.

    Your control

    Log out to end the session. Clear this site’s storage in your browser settings to remove the account. The account record remains after logout so you can sign in again.
