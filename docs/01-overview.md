# Overview

Abdullahi Abukar Ahmed mentors under the name Tripple A. The platform is his public site and the place where students apply for a 3-month mentorship, from beginner to advanced. The mentor and an admin run intake, content, and student progress from dashboards.

The strategy taught in class is Law and Order, compiled by Grand Mentor Abdiwali Moalimuu. That credit stays visible on public pages.

## Facts that do not change

- Sessions are online or in person. Live online sessions use Zoom and Google Meet.
- Pricing starts from 120 USD. A custom price can sit below or above that. The site does not take payment.
- Cohorts are flexible. There is no fixed month on the calendar. The mentor creates a cohort when he is ready and can run more than one at a time.
- A cohort lasts 3 months. The end date is the start date plus 3 months.
- January 2027 is one sample cohort, not a permanent rule.
- Confirmed social link: TikTok https://www.tiktok.com/@tripple.a75. WhatsApp, Facebook, YouTube, Discord, and Instagram stay unpublished until a real URL exists.
- Home stats stay programme facts (3 months, 12 weeks, 2 ways to attend, from 120 USD) until real settings replace them. They are not win rates.

## What the platform is for

- Show the mentor, the strategy, and genuine proof: certificates, trades, and student stories, each with consent.
- Let visitors apply only while a cohort is open.
- Let the mentor publish content without a developer.
- Keep students informed with alerts and a progress view.

## Out of scope

Online payments, hosting live video, live market data, a native app, and public comments. Trade tickers on the site are labelled samples, not a live feed.
